How It Plays

🏍️

Lay Your Wall

Your bike leaves a neon trail behind you. This trail becomes the arena—your weapon and your prison.

🟢

Ride the Green

Green trails are rideable surfaces. Master them, use them, dominate the grid.

🟠

Die on Amber

Amber marks the death line—the lethal edge of your lane. Touch it, and you're out.

⚡

Pierce Through

Break the grid, cut through walls, create new paths. Strategy meets reflex in neon combat.

Synapse Grid is a neon light-cycle racer where the trail you lay becomes the arena. Navigate the grid, master the walls, and survive the amber death lines in this intense solo combat experience.

Built with passion in Godot, this is a high-speed game of reflexes, strategy, and neon chaos.
