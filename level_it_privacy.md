# Level-It Tool - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 23, 2026

Welcome to Level-It Tool. Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application handles user information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Sensor Data**
Level-It Tool uses your device's built-in sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope) solely to calculate and display angles, tilts, and surface levels. This sensor data is processed entirely on your device in real-time. We do not collect, store, or transmit any sensor data to external servers.

**No Personal Data Collection**
We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device ID) on our own servers. 

**Third-Party Services**
The app may utilize standard services provided by Google Play Services or AdMob. These third-party services may collect standard advertising identifiers and performance analytics (like crash logs) to optimize app stability and deliver ads. These services operate under their own privacy policies:
- [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 2. Children's Privacy
Level-It Tool is a general utility app and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

## 3. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

## 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store.
