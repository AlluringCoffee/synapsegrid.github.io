# Synapse Grid
## Neon Bike Combat
**Press kit** · 18 August 2026 · AlluringCoffee / Timothy Bunce

### Logline
Tron-ish bike combat where the trail is a level editor the player is driving.

### Pitch
Your bike lays a neon trail. That trail becomes the arena — weapon and prison. Green walls are rideable. Amber walls kill. The lane edge is the death line. Break the grid, cut through walls, create new paths. Strategy meets reflex in neon combat.

### Features
- **Lay your wall** — the trail you paint is the level you are still driving.
- **Ride the green** — green trails are rideable surfaces.
- **Die on amber** — amber marks the lethal edge of your lane.
- **Pierce through** — cut walls, open paths, box opponents in.
- High-speed solo combat: reflexes plus spatial strategy.
- Built in **Godot 4.2**. Neon light-cycle / bike combat.
